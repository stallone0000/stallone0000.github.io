Qilong Shi (史奇龙)

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Tsinghua University. My research focuses on sketch-based network measurement and data stream mining. I also work on large language models, with a focus on efficient reasoning, reusable reasoning skills, model merging, and supervised fine-tuning. I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science from Peking University in 2023.
